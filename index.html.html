How to play
Waves

Zombies come in waves. Between waves you get an 8 to 12 second break. Press N to skip it.

Chests

Glowing chests give you new guns and ammo. A blinking marker at the edge of the screen points to chests you cannot see. Press F to open one.

Health

Your hearts are in the top right corner. Each heart is 10 HP. Hold right click to wrap a bandage for 5 hearts.

Armor

Your armor slot sits under the hearts. Armor blocks part of every hit until it wears out.

Powers

Every 5 waves the game pauses and you pick 1 of 3 powers. Point at a power icon in the corner to read what it does.

Bosses

Every 10 waves a boss comes, up to 5 at once: the Tyrant, the Butcher, the Witch, the Spitter Queen and the Juggernaut, in that order. Each drops a bandage, ammo and food, and sometimes its own weapon.

Hunger

The drumsticks under your hearts are your hunger. They go down slowly, faster when you sprint. Chests often have food: press Q to eat. If hunger runs out you lose 5 HP every few seconds and the screen goes blurry.

Stamina

Hold Shift to sprint. The yellow bar drains while you run. If it runs out completely you have to wait 10 seconds for it to fill again.

Wandering Trader

Every 7 waves a trader visits during the break. Follow the purple marker and press F. He sells weapons you cannot find anywhere else, with 1000 ammo, and unbreakable armor that blocks 40% of damage. Ammo for his weapons turns up in chests.

Alien invasion

After wave 20 there is a 30% chance aliens invade for 5 waves. They shoot lasers, and their Overlord comes on the 5th wave. Aliens drop crystals. Craft 5 crystals into an Alien Shield at an anvil.

Building

Press B to build. Pick a wood wall, iron wall, spikes or a turret with 1 to 4, then click to place it near you. Zombies chew through walls, so keep them repaired by building new ones.

Levels and skills

Kills and cleared waves give XP. Every level gives a skill point. Spend them in Skills on the menu: Fighter, Survivor and Builder skills.

Pets

Buy a dog or a drone in the Shop under Pets. Your pet follows you every run and attacks zombies.

Weather

From wave 4 a wave can bring a storm (lightning strikes zombies), thick fog, or a blood moon (faster zombies, double coins and XP).

Mini map

The map in the bottom left shows you (white), friends (blue), chests (yellow), the trader (purple), bosses (red) and your buildings.

Daily challenges

Open Daily on the menu to see today's 3 challenges. They give coins and XP and change every day.

Special slots

Next to your weapons are 3 special slots: bandages, food and the alien shield. Only those things go there.

Day and night

Day and night last 2.5 minutes each. It gets darker at night and zombies move a little faster.

Cars

Every map has 2 working cars on the roads. Press F next to one to drive. W drives, S brakes, A and D steer. Hit zombies to knock them away. If the car breaks it explodes, so get out in time. Broken cars come back at the next wave.

Characters

Each character starts with their own weapon and has a small perk, like moving faster or healing more. See them all under Character in the menu.

Rooftops

Tall buildings have a ladder. Walk into it or press F to climb. Zombies can climb too, but slowly.

Move

W A S D or the arrow keys.

Shoot or swing

Aim with the mouse and hold left click.

Bow

Hold left click to pull the string and let go to shoot. When the bar above you is full, the arrow does the most damage.

Bandage

Hold right click, or hold H.

Sprint

Hold Shift while you move.

Eat

Press Q, or click the food slot.

Shield

Hold R to raise the alien shield. It blocks 60% of damage but wears out.

Build

B opens build mode. 1 to 4 picks what to build, left click places it, B or right click stops.

Emotes

Press T and pick an emote with the mouse or 1 to 8. It shows above your head, also for your friends online.

Use

F opens chests, climbs ladders, opens the anvil, gets in cars and talks to the trader.

Inventory

E opens your bag and the crafting book.

Switch weapon

Number keys 1 to 9, the mouse wheel, or click a slot on the weapon bar.

Other keys

N skips the break. P or Esc pauses. M turns sound on or off. The button next to the sound icon (or F11 in the desktop version) switches full screen.

Phone

Drag on the left side to move. Hold on the right side to shoot. Tap Bag for the inventory.

Walker

Slow and steady. Some carry an axe and hit harder.

Runner

Fast, with yellow eyes. Shows up from wave 3.

Archer

Wears a hood and keeps its distance while shooting arrows, even at rooftops.

Blinker

Purple. Teleports next to you every 5 seconds.

Grabber

Grabs you and holds you for 3 seconds, taking 10 HP each second. Kill it to break free.

Brute

Huge and slow, with lots of health. Shows up from wave 5.

Tyrant

The boss that comes every 10 waves. Shoots bone spikes and calls in more zombies.

Butcher

Boss. Swings a cleaver and charges at you very fast every few seconds.

Witch

Boss. Teleports, fires rings of purple hexes and raises zombies.

Spitter Queen

Boss. Slow and bloated. Spits fans of acid at you.

Juggernaut

Boss. Wears thick armor, takes less damage and slams the ground when you get close.

Grey alien

Keeps its distance and shoots green lasers.

Alien trooper

Armored. Fires 3 lasers at once.

Stalker

Fast and almost invisible until it is close.

Alien Overlord

Boss of the invasion. Lasers, laser rings, teleports and calls more aliens. Drops alien weapons and armor.

Patient Zero

The final boss at the end of the World Tour. He can do everything.

Materials

Zombies drop copper (50%), iron (25%) and their axe (50%). Drops fly to you when you get close.

Wood

Hold an axe and swing at a tree. You get 4 to 7 wood when it falls.

String

Swing any sword at a spider web inside buildings or on rooftops. Webs grow back every wave.

Anvil

Every tall building has an anvil on its roof. Stand next to it and press F.

Recipes

Press E and open the Crafting tab. Recipes are sorted into folders. Click one to see what you need and where to find it.

Alien Shield

Aliens drop green crystals. 5 crystals make an Alien Shield at an anvil. Hold R to block 60% of damage until it breaks.

Best weapons

Swords swing fast and hit everything in front of you. The bow shoots far. The mace is slow, but its slam knocks every zombie back.

Endless

Waves never end and zombies get stronger each time. You earn 2 to 5 coins per wave.

Boss Rush

Every wave is a boss fight, and they keep getting stronger. 5 coins per wave.

Hardcore

Endless with tougher zombies and only 3 seconds between waves. Bandages heal less, but you get double coins and XP.

World Tour

20 cities around the world, 10 rounds each. Your progress saves after every city. Beat Patient Zero at the end to save your family.

Multiplayer

Click Multiplayer, then Host game to get a code. Your friends type the code under Join a game. Up to 4 players fight Endless waves together, with 50% more zombies for each extra player. If a friend goes down, stand next to them and hold F for 3 seconds to bring them back. Otherwise they come back when the next wave starts.

Shop

Spend coins on new characters, starting guns and map styles for Endless.

Achievements

Bronze ones are easy, gold ones are medium and red ones are hard. Harder ones give more coins.
