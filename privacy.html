Rustwick Fallen – Privacy Policy

NC Studios · Last updated: November 6, 2026

Short version

Rustwick Fallen does not ask for an account, does not collect your personal information, and does not sell or share any data. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

What is stored, and where

  • Your save (coins, heroes, skins, settings, the nickname you type) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising IDs.

Multiplayer

When you choose to play with friends, your device connects directly to theirs (peer-to-peer). To find each other, the game downloads the PeerJS library from unpkg.com and uses the public PeerJS connection service and Google's STUN servers. Like any internet connection, these services can see your device's IP address while connecting. Your nickname, hero, accessories, and game actions are sent only to the friends in your room. Chat in multiplayer uses preset messages only.

Update check

The game checks a small file on our GitHub Pages website to see if a new version is available. GitHub may log normal web request information (such as IP address) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Ads

The current version has no ads. If ads are added in the future (for example, optional videos for in-game coins), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will name the ad provider.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Contact NC Studios through the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page.
